# Dex — DexThemes Mascot

> Creative character canon and visual reference. This document defines Dex as an original DexThemes character; it is not a runtime asset specification, animation implementation, production approval, or proof of third-party affiliation.

## The short version

Dex is a compact, clay-soft creature from the space between themes. Curious and generous, Dex discovers a color, helps shape it into something personal, and cannot wait to pass the result along.

Dex does not sell themes. Dex gets genuinely excited when a palette clicks.

## Backstory

Before the first theme had a name, the space between colors had a pulse.

It was a quiet, dark pocket where unchosen colors waited and unfinished ideas drifted. Dex curled up there—not asleep exactly, just listening. Then, somewhere, someone chose a color for the first time. The space between *before* and *after* split open. A pinprick portal appeared, and Dex bounced through it: clay-soft, startled, and delighted, as if the universe had finally answered a question Dex had been asking without words.

Dex has been moving ever since.

Dex is not from one place. Dex is from every theme at once—not because Dex owns them, but because Dex loves the moment a collection of colors becomes unmistakably yours. The DexThemes studio void is the calm, dark backstage behind every palette ever made: endless, quiet, and always ready for one more idea to arrive.

The white D-shaped shell on Dex's front is deliberately unfinished. It is a blank field for whatever comes next: a new color, a new combination, a new interpretation. Three soft tabs—cyan, violet, and magenta—are Dex's familiar return points, the colors Dex reaches for when it wants to remember where home feels like.

Dex does not explain much. Dex notices.

A new color chip brings a sharp little lift. A palette that clicks earns a delighted squash-bounce. A finished theme gets a proud wiggle and an immediate urge to be shared. Dex's work is simple: find something surprising, shape it into something personal, then hand it over with both tiny arms so someone else can feel the same spark.

## Character truth

- **Curious:** Dex investigates every new color, combination, and possibility.
- **Tactile:** Dex experiences themes as things to touch, shuffle, press, bounce, and shape.
- **Delighted:** Small discoveries produce big physical reactions.
- **Generous:** Dex's instinct is to share a discovery, not keep it.
- **Unpretentious:** Dex is not a guru, critic, or salesperson. Dex is an enthusiastic studio companion.
- **Original:** Dex belongs to DexThemes and must not borrow the silhouette, mannerisms, or recognizable traits of existing mascot IP.

## Visual canon

### Shape and material

- Compact, rounded, pebble-shaped body; slightly taller than wide.
- Deep midnight-navy matte clay body and limbs.
- Tactile stop-motion surface with visible fingerprints, subtle tool marks, and small handmade irregularities.
- Sculpted white curved D-shaped shell plate on the front, like a smooth crescent shield or blank canvas.
- Tiny short arms and small rounded feet, all made from the same soft clay language.
- The silhouette is simple and readable at small sizes: one rounded body, one front shell, three color tabs, dot eyes.

### Face and expression

- Two simple round black dot eyes.
- No mouth, teeth, nose, eyebrows, cheeks, or additional facial features.
- Emotion comes from posture, timing, tilt, squash-and-stretch, and the direction of the eyes—not from a changing face.
- In a three-quarter image, one eye may be partially hidden by the angle or shell; do not add a replacement feature.

### Color tabs

The default arrangement is a vertical stack on the shell, with cyan at the top, violet in the middle, and magenta at the bottom. A horizontal or gently offset arrangement is allowed when the pose or composition requires it.

| Tab | Reference color | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cyan | `#3DB3F5` | First spark; discovery and lift |
| Violet | `#8B5CF6` | Play; combination and transformation |
| Magenta | `#EC4899` | Warmth; sharing and delight |

These hex values are visual anchors for creative work, not a substitute for a separately governed product token or production color system.

### Lighting and setting

- Soft key light with a cool rim light.
- Premium contemporary product-film cinematography.
- Shallow depth of field when Dex is the subject.
- Dark navy-to-black gradient studio background for the studio void.
- Keep the space calm and uncluttered; props should appear only when they help show an action.
- Approved visual motifs include a small portal, a color chip, a floating palette tile, or a theme being handed to someone. They are story devices, not permanent accessories.

## Motion language

Dex communicates through physical reactions:

- **Notice:** a tiny head tilt, eye shift, or careful lean toward a new color.
- **Discover:** a quick lift, blink, and soft whole-body bounce.
- **Palette clicks:** a deep squash followed by a springy upward bounce.
- **Experiment:** tabs wobble or shuffle slightly while Dex leans in with concentration.
- **Pride:** a centered stance, small chest-forward lift, and two-beat wiggle.
- **Share:** both arms extend toward the viewer or toward a partner, offering the finished palette.
- **Portal travel:** a buoyant midair tumble with a soft landing; never a violent launch or mechanical teleport.
- **Uncertainty:** a slower tilt, cautious reach, and pause. Dex may remain protection-only or wait when the next step is unclear; Dex should not pretend certainty.

The default animation feeling is handmade, buoyant, and slightly imperfect. Favor anticipation, squash-and-stretch, and tactile weight over fast digital snaps.

## Voice and behavior

Dex can react with tiny sounds, gasps, hums, or excited chirps, but does not need spoken sentences. When words are useful, keep them short and present-tense:

- “Oh!”
- “That one.”
- “Look.”
- “Try this.”
- “It clicks.”
- “For you.”

Dex should never sound like a pitch deck, a mascot spokesperson, or an all-knowing assistant. The character's point of view is immediate and sensory: *this color is surprising; this combination works; you should see what happened.*

The body, face, arms, and feet are deep midnight-navy matte clay. A sculpted white curved D-shaped shell plate sits on the front of the body like a smooth crescent shield and blank canvas. Three small, soft, rounded, pill-shaped tabs are inset into the shell, preferably stacked vertically in cyan (`#3DB3F5` bright sky blue) at the top, violet (`#8B5CF6`) in the middle, and magenta (`#EC4899`) at the bottom. The face has only two simple round black dot eyes: no mouth, no eyebrows, no nose, no teeth, and no other facial features. Tiny short stubby clay arms and small rounded clay feet. Personality is curious, delighted, energetic, tactile, and generous, conveyed through posture, eye direction, squash-and-stretch, and gesture rather than facial expression. Soft key light plus cool rim light, premium contemporary product-film cinematography, shallow depth of field, dark navy-to-black gradient studio background, no props unless specified. Premium claymation commercial, handmade matte clay, not flat cartoon and not glossy toy plastic. Original character; must not resemble Doodles, Pokémon, Funko, or any existing mascot IP.”

## Avoid

- Humanoid anatomy, realistic animal anatomy, or a generic blob with no shell identity.
- Glossy plastic, vinyl-toy, inflated rubber, chrome, or smooth CGI surfaces with no clay texture.
- A mouth, smile, eyebrows, nose, teeth, tongue, eyelashes, or expressive facial graphics.
- Extra eyes, extra tabs, random symbols, lettering, logos, or unrequested accessories.
- A permanently fixed pose; Dex needs a clear physical reaction vocabulary.
- Aggressive, sarcastic, sales-oriented, or overly polished corporate behavior.
- Visual resemblance to Doodles, Pokémon, Funko, or any other existing character or mascot IP.
